# Vacation and Sick Leave Policy

This policy describes what applies to vacation and sick leave at Nordljus Consulting AB. The policy is based on the Swedish Annual Leave Act and the Swedish Sick Pay Act, presented in simplified form, together with Nordljus's own benefits.

## Number of vacation days

Under the Swedish Annual Leave Act, all employees are entitled to 25 vacation days per year. At Nordljus, all permanent employees have 30 paid vacation days per year, which is five days more than the law requires.

Fixed-term employees have 25 vacation days per year, calculated in proportion to the length of their employment.

The vacation year follows the calendar year, from 1 January to 31 December.

## Applying for vacation

Vacation is applied for in the HR Portal under "Absence > Vacation" and is approved by the line manager.

- Summer vacation (June–August) must be applied for no later than 31 March.
- Other vacation must be applied for at least four weeks in advance.
- Short leave of one to two days can be applied for with one week's notice.

Employees are entitled to four consecutive weeks of vacation during June–August, unless otherwise agreed. The line manager gives a decision on summer vacation no later than 30 April.

## Saving vacation days

Of the 25 statutory vacation days, the days above 20 may be saved, which means a maximum of 5 days per year. The total number of saved days may not exceed 25. Saved days must be taken within five years.

The five extra days that Nordljus gives beyond the law cannot be saved. They must be taken during the vacation year and are otherwise lost. Days that are not taken and cannot be saved are not paid out in cash while the employment continues.

## Vacation pay and vacation supplement

During vacation, the employee receives their regular monthly salary. In addition, a vacation supplement of 0.8 percent of the monthly salary is paid for each vacation day taken. The vacation supplement is paid with the salary in the month after the vacation.

## Reporting sick

An employee who becomes sick must notify their line manager before 09:00 on the first day of sickness, by phone or Teams. The sick leave is then registered in the HR Portal under "Absence > Sickness".

When the employee has recovered, they must report back in the HR Portal on the same day they return to work.

## Sick pay and qualifying deduction

During the first 14 calendar days of a sick period, Nordljus pays sick pay. Sick pay is 80 percent of the salary. A qualifying deduction ("karensavdrag") is made from the sick pay, equal to 20 percent of an average week's sick pay.

From day 15, the Swedish Social Insurance Agency (Försäkringskassan) pays sickness benefit. The employee applies for sickness benefit from the Swedish Social Insurance Agency themselves. Nordljus does not pay any supplement to the sickness benefit.

If the employee becomes sick again within five calendar days after a previous sick period, it counts as the same sick period and no new qualifying deduction is made.

## Medical certificate

From the eighth calendar day of a sick period, the employee must submit a medical certificate. The certificate is uploaded in the HR Portal. In special cases, the line manager may request a medical certificate from the first day, for example in the case of repeated short-term absence.

## Repeated short-term absence and rehabilitation

If an employee has been absent due to sickness six times or more during a twelve-month period, the line manager books a follow-up meeting. The purpose is to understand the cause and see whether the work needs to be adjusted, not to question the absence.

For sick leave longer than four weeks, the line manager contacts HR to start a rehabilitation plan. Nordljus works with the occupational health service Hälsopunkten for rehabilitation support.

## Sickness during vacation

If an employee becomes sick during their vacation, the vacation days can be exchanged for sick days. This requires that the employee reports sick to the line manager on the first day of sickness and submits a medical certificate for the whole period. The vacation days can then be taken at a later time.

## Contact

Questions about vacation and sick leave should first be directed to the line manager. Questions about salary, vacation supplement and sick pay should be sent to the payroll department at payroll@nordljus.example.
