# Policy on the Use of AI at Work

This policy describes how employees of Nordljus Consulting AB may use AI tools at work, for example chatbots, coding assistants and tools for text, images and transcription. The policy applies to all employees, consultants and interns. The policy is owned by the AI Council.

Rules marked as **Hard rule** must never be broken. A breach of a hard rule is treated as a security incident.

## Approved AI tools

Only AI tools that the AI Council has approved may be used at work. The approved tools are:

- **Nordljus Assistant**, the company's internal AI chat. May be used for all information classes except Strictly Confidential.
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot** with a company account. May be used for Public and Internal information.
- **GitHub Copilot Business** with a company account. May be used for code in Nordljus's own projects and in client projects where the client has given written approval.

Free versions and private accounts of AI tools may not be used for work tasks, even if the tool is otherwise on the list.

## Information classes

All information at Nordljus is divided into four classes:

- **Public**: information that has already been published, for example on nordljus.example.
- **Internal**: information for employees, for example internal routines and meeting notes without client details.
- **Confidential**: client data, contracts, quotes, financial information and personal data.
- **Strictly Confidential**: sensitive personal data (for example health), payroll lists, security information and information that the client has marked as secret.

## Hard rules

1. **Hard rule:** Strictly Confidential information must never be entered into any AI tool.
2. **Hard rule:** Confidential information may only be entered into Nordljus Assistant.
3. **Hard rule:** Personal data about clients, candidates or colleagues must not be entered into AI tools unless it has first been removed or anonymised, with the exception of Nordljus Assistant.
4. **Hard rule:** AI must never make decisions about hiring, dismissal, salary or the assessment of individual people. AI may be used as support, but the decision must always be made by a human.
5. **Hard rule:** Passwords, API keys and other login credentials must never be entered into any AI tool.

## Review of AI-generated content

The employee is always responsible for what they pass on, regardless of whether it was created with AI or not. AI-generated text, code and analysis must be reviewed by a human before it is used.

AI-generated code must go through the same code review as other code. Facts, figures and sources produced by AI must be checked against a reliable source before they are used.

## Labelling and transparency

Deliverables to clients where AI has contributed significantly, for example reports or analyses, must be labelled with a sentence stating that AI has been used as support. AI-generated images must always be labelled.

If a client has prohibited the use of AI in the contract, the client's requirements take precedence over this policy.

## Recording and transcribing meetings

Meetings may be recorded or transcribed with AI only if all participants have been informed and no one has objected. This applies to both internal meetings and client meetings. Transcription may only be done with Microsoft 365 Copilot or Nordljus Assistant.

Meetings concerning HR matters, rehabilitation or salary reviews must never be recorded or transcribed with AI.

## Requesting a new AI tool

Anyone who wants to use an AI tool that is not on the list of approved tools sends a request to IT support through the IT Portal. The request must describe the tool, what it will be used for and what type of information it will handle.

The AI Council assesses the request and gives a decision within 20 working days. The tool may not be used before it has been approved.

## If something goes wrong

If information has accidentally been entered in breach of the hard rules, it must be reported as a security incident to security@nordljus.example on the same day. An employee who reports an incident themselves and in time will not be penalised for the mistake.

## Training

All employees must complete the Nordljus AI training within one month of starting their employment, or within one month of this policy taking effect. The training is available on the intranet under "Training > AI at work" and takes about 45 minutes.
