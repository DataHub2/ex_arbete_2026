# Parental Leave Policy

This policy describes what applies to employees of Nordljus Consulting AB who are going on parental leave. The policy is based on the Swedish Parental Leave Act and the rules of the Swedish Social Insurance Agency (Försäkringskassan), presented in simplified form, together with Nordljus's own benefits that go beyond the law.

## Right to parental leave

All employees have the right to parental leave under the Swedish Parental Leave Act, regardless of type of employment and how long they have been employed. This also applies to employees on probation and fixed-term employees.

An employee may take full-time leave until the child is 18 months old, whether or not they receive parental benefit. After that, the employee has the right to be on leave on the days they receive parental benefit. Employees also have the right to reduce their working hours by up to one quarter until the child turns 8 or has finished the first year of school.

## Parental benefit from the Swedish Social Insurance Agency

Parental benefit is paid by the Swedish Social Insurance Agency (Försäkringskassan), not by Nordljus. Parents have a combined total of 480 days of parental benefit per child. 90 of these days are reserved for each parent and cannot be transferred to the other parent.

Applications for parental benefit are made to the Swedish Social Insurance Agency. HR cannot answer questions about individual cases at the Swedish Social Insurance Agency.

## Notifying parental leave

Parental leave must be reported to the employee's line manager at least two months before the leave starts. If that is not possible, the leave must be reported as soon as possible.

Leave is reported in the HR Portal under "Absence > Parental leave". Enter the start date, end date and extent (full-time or part-time). The line manager approves the request within five working days.

Leave may be divided into a maximum of three periods per calendar year. More periods may be granted if the line manager approves.

## Parental pay supplement from Nordljus

In addition to parental benefit, Nordljus pays a parental pay supplement. The supplement is 10 percent of the monthly salary and is paid for a maximum of 180 calendar days per child.

To be eligible for the parental pay supplement, the employee must:

- have been employed by Nordljus for at least 12 consecutive months when the leave starts,
- receive parental benefit from the Swedish Social Insurance Agency on the days the supplement covers.

The parental pay supplement is paid automatically with the regular salary once the leave has been registered in the HR Portal. The supplement is not paid for days with parental benefit at the minimum level.

## Vacation during parental leave

Parental leave with parental benefit counts as vacation-earning time for up to 120 days per child (180 days for a single parent). This means that the employee earns vacation during that time in the same way as when working. See also the Vacation and Sick Leave Policy.

## Temporary parental benefit (care of a sick child)

When caring for a sick child ("VAB"), the employee must notify their line manager on the same day, no later than 09:00, via Teams or phone. The absence is then registered in the HR Portal under "Absence > Care of child".

Compensation for caring for a sick child is applied for from the Swedish Social Insurance Agency. Nordljus does not pay any supplement for care of a sick child.

## Returning to work

The employee has the right to return to the same or an equivalent position after parental leave. No later than one month before the return, the line manager books a return-to-work meeting to go through work tasks, changes in the team and any need for adjustments.

An employee who wants to end their parental leave early must notify the line manager as soon as possible. The return may be postponed by a maximum of one month if the line manager needs time to plan.

## Contact

Questions about parental leave and the parental pay supplement should be sent to HR at hr@nordljus.example. Questions about parental benefit should be directed to the Swedish Social Insurance Agency.
