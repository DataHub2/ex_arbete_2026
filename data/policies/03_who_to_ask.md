# Who to Ask About What

This guide shows where employees of Nordljus Consulting AB should turn with different questions. The basic rule is to ask your line manager first. If the line manager cannot answer, they refer the question onward according to the guide below.

## Line manager

The line manager is the first point of contact for questions about:

- work tasks, priorities and working hours,
- applying for vacation and other leave,
- reporting sick and reporting care of a sick child,
- performance reviews and salary reviews,
- approval of training courses and conferences.

Performance reviews are held once a year during February and March. Salary reviews are held in April, and the new salary applies from 1 May.

## HR

HR answers questions about employment contracts, policies, parental leave, the parental pay supplement, rehabilitation, the work environment and conflicts in the workplace.

HR can be reached at hr@nordljus.example. Replies are normally given within two working days. HR has drop-in hours every Tuesday from 13:00 to 15:00 at the Stockholm office, in the room Ljusgården.

In the case of a conflict with their line manager, an employee may contact HR directly without first going through the line manager.

## Payroll department

The payroll department answers questions about payslips, payments, the vacation supplement, sick pay, the qualifying deduction, tax and expenses.

The payroll department can be reached at payroll@nordljus.example. Salary is paid on the 25th of each month. If the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday, salary is paid on the closest preceding weekday.

Expenses are reported in the HR Portal under "Finance > Expenses" no later than the 10th of the month after the purchase, with the receipt attached.

## IT support

IT support helps with computers, mobile phones, accounts, passwords, access rights and software.

Requests are registered in the IT Portal at it.nordljus.example. For urgent problems, for example if you cannot log in or suspect that your account has been hacked, call the IT support emergency line on 08-555 010 10. The emergency line is open 24 hours a day.

Requests for new AI tools are also sent to IT support, see the AI Policy.

## Information security

Security incidents must be reported immediately to security@nordljus.example and to the IT support emergency line. Examples of incidents are a lost computer or phone, suspected phishing, an email with sensitive information sent to the wrong recipient, and sensitive information pasted into an AI tool that is not approved.

The Information Security Officer is also the contact for questions about how customer data and confidential information may be handled.

## AI Council

The AI Council consists of representatives from IT, information security, legal and HR. The council is responsible for the AI Policy and decides which AI tools may be used.

Questions about how AI may be used at work should be sent to ai-council@nordljus.example. The council normally replies within five working days.

## Safety representative and union contact

Nordljus has one safety representative per office. The safety representative can be contacted about the physical and psychosocial work environment. Contact details are available on the intranet under "Work environment".

Nordljus has a collective agreement. Questions about the collective agreement can be directed to HR or to the local union representative.

## Whistleblowing

Anyone who wants to report serious wrongdoing, for example corruption, fraud or breaches of the law, can do so anonymously through the whistleblowing channel on the intranet under "Whistleblowing". The channel is managed by an external party. Reports for the whistleblowing channel should not be sent to the line manager or HR.
